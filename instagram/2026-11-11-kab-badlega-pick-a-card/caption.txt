Three cards, face down. Pick yours before you read further.

जब मन पूछे, “कब बदलेगा?” तो कहना, “पता नहीं। पर आज 1, 2 या 3 तो चुन सकती हूँ।”

1 The Sun: a child on a horse, arms wide, no reins. 2 Ace of Wands: new leaves on the wand. 3 Three of Cups: three friends, three cups, raised together.

Comment your number, and save this for the next night you ask again. DM CLARITY and Charu can help you see what your card means for you.
